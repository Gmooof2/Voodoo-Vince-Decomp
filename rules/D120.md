# D120 — A unit with both edges fixed by retail evidence is cut even when one outside proc's single copy is stray (in a gap or bracketed by two units); the stray stays uncut with its own reason; edge pins must lie in the unit's anchor gap
id: D120 · group: D · status: superseded-by:D121 · tags: units,delink · origin: 4.4 T5 question, developer 2026-11-04; amends D97 · added: 2026-11-04

## Text

D97 stands except its outside-proc clause. A unit's range edges are fixed only from retail evidence, as D97 lists (LIS anchor, neighbour, window, `chain`, `chain-neighbour`, or a `disasm-verified` pin of the unit's own edge proc whose VA lies in that unit's anchor gap; a pin elsewhere in the file never fixes an edge). An outside proc is explained as `moved` (single copy between two anchors of one other unit), or, when both edges are fixed by that evidence, as `stray`: its single retail copy lies in a gap between unit ranges or is bracketed by two other units. A `stray` proc no longer blocks its unit: the unit is cut on its fixed range and the stray proc stays uncut with its own `delink_reason` naming its copy's VA and what was searched. Any other unexplained outside proc still keeps the unit uncut. Guards kept: each re-cut leaves every already-delinked object's per-object hash unchanged (else `review`); the first unit's yield under 0.4 matches per freed row returns `question` (D8).
