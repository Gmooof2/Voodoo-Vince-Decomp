# D97 — A retail unit range is fixed only from retail evidence
id: D97 · group: D · status: superseded-by:D120 · tags: units,delink · origin: Phase 3.2 T1 binding (filed 3.5 T11) · added: 2026-10-30

## Text

A retail unit range edge is fixed only from retail evidence: LIS anchor, neighbour, window, `chain` (own procs masked-equal at the proto-predicted VA and the only copy in their anchor gap), `chain-neighbour` (the chain closes on the adjacent unit's anchor or disasm-verified pin), or a `disasm-verified` pin of the edge proc. A `boundary` pin only closes a chain's far side; `site-corr`/`unverified` pins and a prototype offset alone never fix an edge (D78). An outside proc is explained only as `moved` (single copy between two anchors of one other unit); else `delink_reason` keeps it and names what was searched.
