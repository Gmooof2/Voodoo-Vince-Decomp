# M30 — pin_rows accepts a vtable pin as corr-verified when every slot is named with an accepted status and the slot count equals the prototype's (an amendment of D113's vtable route; frees about 23 rows; landed in T2 with its control first)
id: M30 · group: - · status: active · tags: ratified · origin: phase 5.3 plan · added: 2026-11-07

## Text

A vtable pin (`??_7`) is accepted as `corr-verified` by pin_rows when every slot of the table is named with an accepted status (`disasm-verified`, `corr-verified` or `near-corr`, D115) and the slot count equals the prototype's. It amends D113's vtable route (slot bytes) for tables whose slots are all named. Not covered: a table with an unnamed or `site-corr` slot, a count mismatch, a twin or dispatch slot the route refuses, or a slot whose own base cannot be shown (each fails closed).
Implementation: `tools/pin_rows.py` `vt_slots`, `slot_names`, `slot_own`, `slot_rivals`, checked in `corr_route` (R5.5-017).
Controls (`tools/gate_controls.py`): pin-slotnames-unnamed, pin-slotnames-site-corr, pin-slotnames-count, pin-slotnames-twin, pin-slotnames-dispatch, pin-slotnames-own-base, pin-vtable-prefix.
Known limits: the base-class table can hide `slot_own` (5.5 D126 review, R5.5-013 finding 5); 6 vtables stay walls with 27 slot hand rows (5.5 T7).
