# D136 — pin_rows bracket anchors only use procs whose retail address lies inside the module's own unit code. (built in 5.5 T4)
id: D136 · group: - · status: active · tags: ratified · origin: phase 5.5 plan (filed as T19) · added: 2026-11-10

## Text

pin_rows' bracket route anchors a row only on procs whose retail address lies inside the module's own unit code range (`config/retail/units.toml`); a proc of another unit never bounds a bracket. A bracket with an anchor outside the unit, a planted body outside the bracket, more than one hit, a deleting destructor whose callee is not its own `??1` (I58) or a callee-down conflict is refused.
Implementation: `tools/pin_rows.py` anchor filter in `bracket`, `dtor_not_own`, `callee_down` (R5.5-017).
Controls: pin-bracket-outside, pin-bracket-ambiguous, pin-bracket-own-dtor, pin-bracket-outside-unit, pin-bracket-anchor-unit, pin-bracket-callee-down, pin-bracket-dtor-untabled, pin-bracket-unit-range.
Known limits: callee-down applies to bracket rows only (5.5 D126 review, R5.5-013 findings 6, 7, 12).
