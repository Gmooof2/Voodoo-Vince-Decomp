# D125 — A unit with both edges fixed by retail evidence is cut when every unexplained outside proc is stray, each left out of the cut unit and recorded beside it; a re-cut's name-only change passes without review
id: D125 · group: D · status: active · tags: units,delink,ratified · origin: 4.5 close, developer 2026-11-06; supersedes D121 · added: 2026-11-06

## Text

D121 stands with clause 1's stray wording made exact. A unit with both edges fixed by D120's evidence is cut when every unexplained outside proc is `stray` (single retail copy in a gap between unit ranges, or bracketed by two other units), whatever their count. Each stray is left out of the unit being cut (its range and ownership); this is not "outside every cut": a stray may lie inside a neighbouring unit's live delink range and be exported with it (HouseOfMirrors creationFunc 0x00070263 in bakeryMain's range). Each stray's VA, original unit, what was searched and its containing cut, if any, are recorded beside the unit in config/retail/units.toml. Any other unexplained outside proc still keeps the unit uncut. Clause 2 (the re-cut guard, name-only changes proven against pre-cut copies) and the yield guard are unchanged from D121.
