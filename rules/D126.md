# D126 — The gate review runs once per generation: the deep-thinker plus another blind reviewer, one merged stop, then locked fixes each with a control shown failing first
id: D126 · group: D · status: active · tags: gate,review,validation,ratified · origin: 4.5 close, developer 2026-11-06; supersedes D107 · added: 2026-11-06

## Text

Once per generation, in its validation phase, the gate review runs as 4.5's did. The deep-thinker gets the brief in `docs/ops/gate_review.md` (the re-running expert refreshes its line numbers and counts first), and at least one other blind reviewer runs beside it (the thinker on a scoped part; the developer's external audit when he runs one, his time and his call); none sees another's findings. No gate or locked-file fix lands between them. One merged review stop checks every finding against the code and gives it a verdict (`gap` / `not a gap` / routed). Then locked `review: yes` fix tasks follow, each real gap with a self-test control first shown DID NOT FAIL on the unfixed tree, then failing as expected.
