## Attempt curve

Per symbol: its attempt rows sorted by `ts` (stable; ledger order breaks ties), up to and including the first `rc` 0 row. Matched at k: that first rc 0 row is row k and the symbol is in `config/matched_set.toml`; otherwise (open, or rc 0 but not in matched_set) the symbol is censored (stopped) at its last counted row.
Lane: `derived/candidates/candidates.jsonl` `lane` joined on the rows' va (`none`: in no lane).
- at risk(k): symbols with >= k counted rows and no match before k; hazard P(match at k | none before) = matched at k / at risk(k).
- raw eventual: (matched at j > k) / (at risk(k) - matched at k), `-` when the denominator is 0.
- censored: 1 - prod over j > k of (1 - hazard(j)) (Kaplan-Meier eventual match after k failures).
