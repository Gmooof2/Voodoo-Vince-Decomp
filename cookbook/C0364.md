# Recount a tool's internal figures without editing it: call it and read its locals

When a close needs a figure a generated doc does not print (5.2 T15: fresh settled bodies of the lane-sizing classes,
C0310's trap), and the tool is the only correct source of the predicates, do not copy its logic into a scratch script.
1. Import the tool (`tools/` on sys.path) and call the one function with exactly the inputs its `main` passes.
2. Capture that function's locals at return: `sys.setprofile(f)` where `f(frame, event, arg)` stores
   `dict(frame.f_locals)` when `event == "return" and frame.f_code is tool.func.__code__`; unset after the call.
3. Count from those locals (row classes, settled bodies, `unit_at`, the "named" set), and print a sum check against
   the figures the generated doc already shows (5.2 T15: k1 37 + D115 575 = 612, bodies 581) before trusting any new one.
Keep it scratch under `.run/<phase>/`; if a figure is needed at every close, add it to the tool's section instead.
Trap: locals are the function's final state; a variable reused in a loop holds only its last value.
