# Size an unsized data pin from the PDB: match S_LDATA32/S_GDATA32 by address, size by tpi

Problem: a data pin row with no size, compared proto against retail over "next proto public minus this one", overshoots into the next object's bytes and differs at the tail (3 of 13 rows in 4.5 T16.1.c2). Bounding by the next retail-side name did not help (1940, 552, 116 bytes).

Move (T16.1.c3, `tools/pin_rows.py` `data_equal`):
- The extent is the min of three: the next proto public minus P (capped at EXT_CAP), the next retail-side name, and the PDB data type size at P when there is exactly one size.
- Get the PDB size this way: `pdb_info.read_streams` / `iter_records` over the DBI symbol-record stream, taking S_LDATA32/S_GDATA32 and their `_ST` forms, then `pdb_types.load_tpi` / `tsize` on the record's type.
- Match by address (segment:offset to P), never by name: static members are named `Class::member` in the records, not by their decorated public.
- Mask the proto `.reloc` dwords on both sides. Result: 13 of 13 rows equal in 0.05 s. A +4 shift fails `route-global:data-differs`.
- Rows sized this way: `_inflate_mask` 68, `g_strMediaPath` 512, `_delayMusicName` 28; their first differences were at +76, +520 and +92.

Also: a near-corr plant needs a row with site (0,0,0). Moving a row that has sites turns its own sites into conflicts, so `site-conflict-only` fires first.
