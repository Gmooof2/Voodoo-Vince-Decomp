# M18 callee-name-conflict on an XDK thunk: pin its outgoing target first
- Symptom: M18 callee check (`name_sites.callee`) FAILs `callee-name-conflict` for an XDK thunk like `_IDirectSoundBuffer_Pause@8` (0x00179A7C, 0x1C) because its outgoing call (+0x15) lands on a target named only by xbsymdb undecorated (`CDirectSoundBuffer_Pause`), not by the PDB public.
- Mechanism: naming gap, not a byte mismatch. Run M18 on the outgoing target with its decorated PDB public (`?Pause@CDirectSoundBuffer@DirectSound@@QAGJK@Z` 0x001790A8 0x51); it passes; pin it; the outer thunk then passes and is pinned.
- A callee whose proto body differs in length from retail (`_D3DDevice_Clear@24` proto 1000 vs retail 0x2C9) fails `not masked-equal`: no pin; its caller stays provisional (SwampRenderer::ClearScreen, objdiff 100, bytes masked 1).
- After pinning, `delink.py --fresh` (~195 s) and `relink.py cut` (~341 s) regenerate; cut relocs then read `DSOUND::_IDirectSoundBuffer_Pause@8` etc.
