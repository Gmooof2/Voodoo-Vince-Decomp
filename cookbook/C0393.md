# Parallel cl 13.10 build: proving byte-identical objs

5.4 T5.c4 (build.py pool):
- cl writes a COFF TimeDateStamp (bytes 4..8) into every obj, so two serial builds differ in every obj. Compare with those 4 bytes zeroed.
- Each obj embeds its own output path, so two builds compare equal only when both write to the same out dir.
- Give each job its own scratch dir (cwd and TMP/TEMP), removed after. Keep `built` and `failures` in job order: `ThreadPoolExecutor.map` keeps it, completion order is never used.
- cl.exe peaks at 15 MB max (9 MB median) across 216 sources; the compiler stages are DLLs inside it. 32 MB/job gives about 2x margin.
- The build drops from 6.3 s to 1.3 s alone at 8 workers; inside the gate it drops from 11.3 s to 2.9 s. The gate's total is set by its longest lane (audit).
- Control: the fixture built at workers 1 and pooled gives equal {relpath: sha256 with the stamp zeroed}; two planted failures come out in source order in both.
