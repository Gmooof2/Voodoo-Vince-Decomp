# Accessor or direct member: `lea ecx,[eax+N]` vs `add eax,N; mov ecx,eax` tells which

- 4.3 T5.c9, `CeilingFanMain::HitBy` 0x00131FB6 (/O1): seven forms through the PDB inline `SwampScript::GetName()` stuck at 91.58; the inline turned retail's `add eax,4; mov ecx,eax` into `lea ecx,[eax+4]`. Reading `->_name` directly matched (draft 9).
- Counterpart: T5.c4 `LocatorManager::Load` 0x0010AB9B: `x->_script` read twice scored 92.35; the PDB inline `Locator::GetScript()` gave retail's single load (C0149's accessor move).
- So neither form is the default: retail `add`+`mov` on a member address = direct member; one shared load where source reads twice = accessor.
