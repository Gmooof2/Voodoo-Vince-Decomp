# Fold-VA relink with several template copies at one folded address (4.3 T2.c1, scratch prototype)

Use: our objects emit COMDAT copies under different fold-alias names at one pinned VA (`??4ResourceHandle<Texture/VertexShader>` at 0x1819A; `~ResourceHandle<...>` at 0x292A6); relink refuses `our ranges overlap at 0x<va>` (tools/relink.py:1034).

- Accept in `same()` only when every name is a `func` line of symbols_pin.txt at that VA, size, bytes and Selection equal, relocs to externals compared by resolved target VA (names differ per template arg). One object or two.
- A matched row's section comes from its `defs` (rows carry no `num`); sort rows first at a tied VA so the matched copy is kept.
- Rename the extra name's COMDAT in EVERY object of ours that defines it (`<name>@fold_<va8>`, dumped copy; /OPT:REF drops it), not only the hole's object: a strong copy elsewhere (BumperCar.obj) beats the weak alias and lands at the image end (`.text vsize ... does not give XBE ...`).
- That object's DIR32/REL32 references move to a fresh undefined `<name>`, bound weak to the kept copy via weak.obj (T6.1 path).
- Negative control: flip byte 0 of one copy -> still exit 2 `our ranges overlap`.
- A decl-only `template<>` specialization that suppressed a copy (C0154) blocks the row's match (ClearStateCache 77.54 with SwampRenderer.cpp:223 kept): remove it once the relink accepts the copy.
- Scratch clone: two files checked out CRLF made `verifier_lock.py write` refuse; normalise to LF (C0006).
