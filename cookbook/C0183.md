# C0183 — Retail VA low nibble refutes /O2 /Ot: /Ot section align 16, /Os align 1
tags: msvc,opt,control,o2,os,ot,coff,section-align · date: 2026-11-01 · phase/task: 4.1/T10 · origin: T10 opt controls

# The /O2 default leaves a mark outside the bytes: section alignment (extends C0065)

- Same source, one flag (4.1 T10, GameRegions.cpp): `/O1 /Oy` gives `.text` align 1, `/O2 /Oy` gives align 16 (Diary.cpp under `/O2 /Oy`: 16 too), `/O2 /Os /Oy` gives align 1. So the mark follows `/Ot` vs `/Os`, not `/O2` itself.
- A function compiled with `/Ot` must land on a 16-byte boundary, so a retail VA whose low nibble is non-zero refutes `/O2` with its default `/Ot`, even when the body byte-passes the `/O2` control (leaf rows such as `?Message@AreaLoadRegionMain@@`, C0065 line 8).
- Limit: `/O2 /Os` stays open; it differs from `/O1` only by `/Oi` (C0087). A body with no intrinsic cannot separate them.
- Population, consistent with no `/Ot` in game code: 40 of 615 matched VAs have a zero low nibble (6.5%; chance 6.25%). Refutes per row; a nibble of zero proves nothing.
- Read alignment: bits 20-23 of the section Characteristics in the COFF header, `1 << (v-1)`; `--listing` keeps the match_one workdir, a passing run without it wipes the object. The delinked retail object has no alignment value (0), so read the retail VA.
