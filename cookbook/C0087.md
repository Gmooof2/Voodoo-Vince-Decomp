# Inlined memcpy (rep movsd + rep movsb) in an /O1-looking body: add /Oi

- Symptom: retail body copies with `rep movsd` then `rep movsb` (memcpy intrinsic); our `/O1 /Oy` build calls `_memcpy` (objdiff 64.5 on `_inflate_flush`).
- Fix: unit opt `/O1 /Oy /Oi`. `/O2 /Os /Oy` gives the same bytes (equally 100), as does `/Og /Oi /Os /Oy /Gs /Gy`; `/O2 /Oy` and `/Ox` fail (60.9).
- Implication: BeepLib zlib units may be built `/O2 /Os` (`_zcfree`, `_inflateInit2_`, `_inflate_blocks_new`, `_inflate_codes_new` also pass under it); units without intrinsics cannot tell `/O1` from `/O2 /Os`. The `/O2 /Oy` control alone does not settle it.
- Find it: llvm-objdump the delinked cut and `.run/<phase>/match_one/build/*.obj` side by side (C0063).
- Source: 2.5 T5.c2 (infutil.c `inflate_flush`, units.toml:1430).
