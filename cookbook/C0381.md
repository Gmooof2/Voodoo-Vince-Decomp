# Defined-callee cleanup, Identity hand form, ??_G of a fold row

- `add esp,4` vs `pop ecx` after a 1-arg cdecl call (cl 13.10 /O1): with only a declaration of the callee in the TU, cl emits `pop ecx` in every statement order (T9.c1: 719 permutations of UIBeadMain's ctor). Retail's `add esp,4` appears when the callee is defined (not inlined) in the same TU. Fix: define the callee's real body in the unit; if that callee is itself a fail row, both rows unguard together. `noinline` gives the same effect but is barred (D116).
- Matrix::Identity (memset + 4 fld1 diagonal stores) never inlines at /Os x87, not even into a one-line caller; Zero + 3 stores does. D117 hand form matches retail: `it->Zero(); it->row[0].x = it->row[1].y = it->row[2].z = it->row[3].w = 1.0f;` with a `// !FAKE:` line (VinceGiblets ctor 0x00078D86). Revises C0380's "Identity wall".
- Matching a ctor emits its `??_G`. When retail's `??_G` is another unit's fold row, ours must not be longer, or relink fails `lies in a replaced range` naming a third unit's external. A declared `virtual ~X() {}` adds a vptr store the implicit dtor lacks (VinceGiblets: 47 B vs 41 B): leave the dtor implicit when retail has no `??1X`.
- probe_form under MSYS_NO_PATHCONV=1: pass `--spec` as a Windows path (C:/...).
