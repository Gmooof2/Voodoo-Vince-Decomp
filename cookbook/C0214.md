# Stack-slot layout levers at 129-256 B (4.3 T8 W1): inner blocks, comma expressions, preloaded x87 default

- Frame size or slot order off while instructions match: the slot of a temporary is shared when its lifetime ends. An inner `{ }` block around the temp lets a later copy reuse its slot (T8.c4 GatorNode::SetWorldPosition, 12-byte locals followed neither declaration order nor scope).
- Sibling blocks share stack slots; one statement shares nothing with the next. A comma expression keeps two temps live in one full-expression (T8.c1 SwitchCryptMain::Activate: frame 0x50 vs retail 0x5C until `Normalize` through `v4 = &...GetWorldPosition()` was one comma expression, the sum its own statement; `!FAKE`).
- x87 default loaded before a branch: `float v = 1.0f; if (c) v = expr;` (T8.c6 HUDUI).
- Compare tells after `fnstsw ax`: `test ah,5; jp` is `<`, `test ah,0x41; jp` is `<=` (C0088).
- Retail negating a returned Vector3 (load z,y,x, store x,y,z): the C0191 pointer local `const Vector3* p = &_node->GetFacing();` plus a named `Vector3 v1(-p->x, -p->y, -p->z);`; a temporary argument stays at 90.0 (T8.c3 BasketCase).
