# Null audit values break two gate self-test controls

Symptom: `gate.py --self-test` ends `57 failed as expected of 59`; `audit` and `audit-env` print `DID NOT FAIL as expected at audit_public (rc 1, magic hit)` though the verdict line is `FAIL audit_public: fail: rc 1: ...`.

Mechanism: `_tool` keeps only the last 3 output lines as the verdict (gate.py:153). With `xbox_ip`, `boot_folder` or `original_install` null, the audit prints `not checked` lines on stderr after the HIT line, and they fill those 3 lines. The control looks for `HIT paths magic <path>`. The audit did its job; only the control's reading misses.

Move: give the three keys values, real or made up, that occur nowhere in the tree: the classes then run, and the self-test is 59 of 59. Do not spell a made-up value in a tracked file: the audit hits it. A real fix touches a locked tool, so it needs a review.

Superseded for the verdict tail at 4.1 T3.1: the tail drops `not checked` lines, null keys stay null in a public tree; 4.1 T12 clone 75 of 75, validation V4.1-W5. The Move above is history.

Evidence: 3.6 T14, public clone: 57 of 59 with null, 59 of 59 with made-up values, same tree; also `docs/ops/validation.md` V3.6-8.
