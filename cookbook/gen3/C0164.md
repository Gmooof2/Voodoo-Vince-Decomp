# Counting comments in src: line-anchored greps miss most of them

`^\s*//` counts comment-start lines only: R3.6-005's 3238 lines against 10401 real comment lines, 7140 of them trailing on code. And a prefix class like `// 0x..` calls a prose note annotation (931 such lines, 870 `// 0x.. (...)`). Result: 1030 residue by grep, 3052 free-text lines by a real scan.

Move: scan with `provenance_lint.comment_spans` (string literals skipped, block comments handled), classify the whole comment text with fullmatch-style regexes, and reconcile against the grep in the log. `tools/readability_census.py` does this. Same for `// !FAKE:`: a raw grep (189) also catches prose and commented-out code; a label is a comment whose text starts with it (187).
