# New-unit matching (LevelLoader, 5.2 T3.c3): finish traps and small forms
- Before a batch finish, check every callee's pin status: a row calling through a `site-corr` pin is refused by the gate's pin_rows layer even when match_one shows objdiff 100 + bytes match (GetLevelStats 0x00105BED -> push_back<LevelStats> 0x00105BA2, symbols_pin.txt:3910). Leave it with its verdict; it frees when the callee pin is disasm-verified (C0264).
- `return cond ? true : false;` gives retail's setcc / `sbb al` bool returns.
- memcmp three-way (`repe cmpsb`) under `/Oi`: hold the result in an `int` local and `#include <string.h>`; a hand declaration of memcmp fails provenance_lint. A row needing `/Oi` is the unit's opt control evidence (C0137): re-run every matched row of the unit under the new opt.
- Private class statics mangle `@@0`; declaring them public renames the relocation and objdiff masks it.
- VC7.1 `vector` iterators are nested classes, not raw pointers.
- Rows walled by a pin disabled at the cut (R8) wait for that pin: list them per disabled VA in the cut's log.
