# Merge sibling blocks to pool temps into retail's frame size; where a register stand-in is defined decides a spill

- Retail frame larger than ours with temps sharing: merging two sibling blocks (an Apply block and an operator* block) into one block pooled their temps and gave retail's frame (KosmoBotHeadMain::AnimationTrigger 0x0004AE71: 0xb4 -> 0xc0, 99.54 -> 99.90). Pointer-to-temporary and inner-block forms brought back the old pooling. Opposite direction of C0333/C0335 (splitting): pick by whether ours is bigger or smaller.
- An inline by-value `Apply` is not inlined in /O1-only units and blocks `operator+` inlining after it.
- A register stand-in (`BeepTDF* v4 = sub;`) placed inside the success branch moved registers (BeepTDF::ParseRawData 0x00034FE2 94.82 -> 97.32); defined before the call it made cl load early and spill another value.
