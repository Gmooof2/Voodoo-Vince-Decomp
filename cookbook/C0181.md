# One row wants an intrinsic the unit's opt lacks; an opaque class's alignment moves its stack temp
- 4.1 T8.c2, `RegionManager::NotifyMatchingRegions`: retail inlines `strlen` under `/O1 /Oy` (no /Oi). `#include <string.h>` plus `#pragma intrinsic(strlen)` at file scope matched; the unit's opt and its 24 listed rows stayed as they were. Alternative to C0087's unit-wide /Oi when the unit's other rows pass without it.
- 4.1 T8.c2, `ChopperMain::Activate`: opaque `basic_string` as `char _pad00[0x1C]` has align 1, so its temp sat at ebp-29: objdiff 99.88. Real layout members (`_Mysize`, `_Myres`) give align 4 and the match. Same cause as C0094, seen here as a stack offset rather than a size assert.
