# x87 term order, clamps, ctor arg order, access letters (4.3 T8 W5)

- Dot or cross product term order under /O1: cl orders the products by an internal key, not by source order. Permuting the terms did nothing in 4 rows (T8.c9 CheckActivationRange, c25 Frustum::IsVisible, TransformPlane, GetPlaneEquation). What moved the order was the operand's kind: a named local, a reference-bound temporary, or a pointer local (C0191). Try those before permuting. (c8's two MusicRegion rows did change with source order, probably because their operands' kind differed.)
- Clamp to zero: `0.0f > x ? 0.0f : x` gives retail's `fldz; fcomp`; `x < 0.0f ? 0.0f : x` stays at 91.10 (T8.c22 DemoLauncher::FrameTick).
- `Vector3 v(f(), g(), h())` evaluates its arguments right to left. For retail's x, y, z call order, write three member assignments (T8.c27 BeadMain::SetupVelocity 81.46 -> 100).
- Access letters: `Q` public, `I` protected, `A` private non-virtual; `U`, `M` and `E` are the virtual ones. `MAE` means protected virtual: a "not defined" refusal can be the wrong access section (T8.c26 CaptainBrusqueMain::SetState).
- `_node` reloaded before each call where retail reads it once into esi: use a neutral `!FAKE` pointer local `GatorNode* p = (GatorNode*)_node;` or the PDB accessor (T8.c26, c24; C0213).
- Wall: retail radius math through inline asm (`rsqrtss`, `fcompi`, `fcmovbe`, `ffree`) with no `and esp,-16` is not an intrinsic. D116 forbids `__asm`, so guard it (T8.c25 Frustum::IsVisible(AABox) 68.22).
- Git Bash: `--control "/O1 ..."` needs `MSYS_NO_PATHCONV=1`, or the flag becomes a path.
