# Name unresolved reloc targets of identical pool rows by prototype site correspondence

Tool: `PY tools/name_sites.py [--unit U] [--apply] [--control positive|planted]` (3.1 T5).
1. Row identity is re-checked first: proto_map.mask_proc on default.exe at protoVA and on the XBE at va, same mask
   (off, kind) list and equal unmasked bytes. A wrong protoVA fails here (`--control planted`).
2. Same offset, both sides: rel -> va+off+4+rel32, abs -> the dword. Proto target -> PDB public at exactly that VA
   (seg/off -> imageBase + PE section[seg-1].va + off). Zero or several publics -> residue, never a pin (D44).
3. Status is the evidence: `disasm-verified` only when the retail target bytes are checked (callee masked-equal at
   the retail VA, every vtable slot's proc masked-equal, literal bytes through NUL, `__real@<hex>` value).
   Unchecked (globals, near-identical callees) -> status `site-corr`: candidates does not resolve it.
4. `--apply` appends to config/retail/symbols_pin.txt; then `delink.py --fresh`, `candidates.py --write`,
   `pool_buckets.py` (the reloc buckets read candidates `checks.reloc`, the cut only for extent).
Traps:
- XBE section headers mark every section executable (.rdata 0x6, .data 0x7): compare section names, take the kind
  (code vs data) from the prototype PE flags.
- candidates lists retail immediates in the low image range (base 0x10000, e.g. push-buffer constants 0x40300) as
  unresolved abs sites. They are constants, not addresses: unnameable, and the row's mask lists differ from the
  prototype (residue `proto-mismatch`, 34 rows at 3.1 T5).
- Positive control compares PDB-sourced names only: ghidra/xbsymdb names are undecorated (`XMemFree` vs a public).
- Masked-equal is weak for tiny callees: a 5-byte `jmp rel32` thunk compares on its E9 opcode alone. The check also
  requires the callee's outgoing targets to agree by name where retail names them, and a callee under 8 unmasked
  bytes needs one agreeing target (T5.c2: 9 short callees downgraded, vtables 40 -> 15 checked: shared tiny slot procs).
