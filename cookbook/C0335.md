# By-reference locals hold their slot for their whole scope; block-scoped temps per call share later slots

- A local passed by reference to an out-of-line callee (`Apply(..., tmp)`) keeps its stack slot for its whole scope; retail's inlined by-value helper frees it. Give each call its own block-scoped `tmp` (`// !FAKE:`) so it can share the slot of a variable declared later; hoist PDB locals that retail fills early to function scope (filled with the inline `Set`).
