# Bracket-pinned rows: codegen exact, walls are unnamed data/callees; sibling bodies confirm by own-class callee

Context: 5.3 T12.1 bracket pilot (D128), 50 bodies in 23 delinked units, 6 coders.

- Most bracket rows reach objdiff 100 with no instruction difference on the first draft; they stay `provisional (masked N)`
  only because a reloc target has no retail name: `??_7<Class>@@6B@` vtables (SwampScript-derived ctors), `??_C@` string
  literals (Ghidra `s_<text>_<addr>`), `__real@` floats at a second retail VA, in-unit `?Init@<C>` callees,
  `__ehhandler$` funclets of VC 7.1 `_Buynode`, out-of-line `_Erase`/`??_G_Node`. The lift is a pin, never a source edit.
- A virtual `??_G`/`??_E` row is emitted only with the vtable (its ctor), so it needs the ctor as companion (C0320);
  flip the ctor guard for one run (C0319) to prove `??_G` codegen first. `??_G` is the emitted name, `??_E` its alias.
- An unrowed ctor whose vtable `??_E` slot is an unnamed retail fold breaks relink (our `??_G` adds .text): keep it guarded.
- Implicit derived dtor tail-jumps the grandparent dtor when the shell base declares no dtor: declare the PDB `virtual ~Base();`.
- Explicit STL instantiation pulls callee bodies into our obj: make a callee with its own row decl-only (C0132), else
  relink overlap or .text vsize drift.
- Same-module byte-identical siblings (deleting dtors, std helpers): the body is confirmed when its relocation targets
  are the pinned row's own-class callees (`??1Oven` 0x7B153 vs `??1OvenMain` 0x7B39C), read with va_dis obj or retail
  disasm; bytes-equal + PASS relink then confirms by name.
- match_one: `--task` takes `T<n>` only (T12.1 refused); a passing run wipes its workdir (use `--listing` or build.py to
  keep an obj for va_dis); provenance_lint refuses typedef names in explicit instantiations and non-PDB locals other
  than v<N>/tmp/i/p; placement `operator new` inside `namespace std {}` hides `::operator new` (C2660).
