# Classify prototype functions in a later build: identical / ambiguous / near-identical / absent

- Identity: masked-byte match (C0010) with the PE `.reloc` HIGHLOW sites inside each proc added to the mask. This fixes the jump-table misses: the prototype control went from 11 misses to 0 (1.2/T9). No minimum length: short procs that hit 2 or more places are honestly ambiguous, and the hit list is capped (32) so 1-byte `ret` procs stay fast.
- Exclusions with a reason: zero-length; thunk (the body is one unconditional jmp); data-in-text (iced decode errors).
- Anchors: take the longest increasing subsequence of (protoVA -> targetVA) over the unique hits, in proto order. On retail, 4938 of 5134 uniques were order-consistent. Out-of-order uniques are chance hits or reordered objects, so they never bound a window.
- Window: the target gap between a proc's neighbouring anchors (prev anchor VA + len, next anchor VA). When the anchors sit in different exec sections, use two ranges, each clipped to its section.
- Candidates: decode each gap once (iced). A candidate is the gap start or an instruction start after a terminator (ret, unconditional jmp, int3) once int3/nop padding is skipped.
- Similarity: 100 * difflib ratio (autojunk=False) over tokens. A token is the iced `code` plus registers plus mem base/index/scale plus unmasked imm/disp. Branch targets (short and near) and in-image abs values become wildcards. Prefilter with real_quick_ratio/quick_ratio against the current best. The whole retail run took about 12 s.
- Traps: a decoy window "gap + k" is often empty, because adjacent anchors leave no gap; sample non-empty gaps instead. Independent best picks collide: on retail, 184 procs shared 57 candidate VAs. Assign one-to-one within a gap, greedily by similarity.
- Similarity is never match evidence (D81); every emitted VA is tagged source/sha1/unverified (D42).
- Threshold (1.2/T9 retail): decoy p50 16.67 against real p50 92.31. The share of decoys at or above the cut is 4.2% at 70 and 0.6% at 85, and the risk sits in procs under 64 tokens (decoy p99 about 85-89; for 64 tokens and up, 41). Expected false positives = decoy rate x truly-absent count, not x the near count: at 70 that is about 11 of 1260.
