# Pointer local taken just before a local's first read moves its slot (liveness packing); __forceinline evidence is D117-barred

- Where the PDB has no block scopes yet retail locals share slots, packing is by liveness. `const Vector3* p = &planePt1;` taken just before planePt1's first read (C0191) moved it into normal0's slot as retail (TransformPlane 0x000DE1FC); block scopes, decl order, names and const-ref did nothing.
- A PDB-recorded by-value `Matrix::Apply` stays out of line at /O1 and /Ob1 (no opt lever).
- `__forceinline` operator-/operator^ made a cross product byte-exact (GetPlaneEquation 0x000DE0F6) but is a D117-barred form: record it as evidence only, never keep it; a probe of a barred form still costs a ledger attempt unless run through the free probe loop (C0345).
- Changing one term's form (a 4-arg Vector4 ctor) changed another term's x87 order: the ordering key is function-global (C0341).
