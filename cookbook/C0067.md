# cl 13.10 /O1 bit-test forms: `test byte imm` from plain-int flags + LF_ENUM masks

- 6 bittest rows (2.2 T6): zero `bt*`; zero LF_BITFIELD in the types touched. Every flag = plain `unsigned int`/`int` member tested with a mask from an LF_ENUM (class `__unnamed` enums) or a CRT macro.
- Single-bit mask < 0x100 on a memory member -> `test byte ptr [reg+off],imm` (MoviePlayer::Play `_flags & RENDER_TEXTURE` 0x10 @0x14; SoundNode::DoNotifyParent `_flags & NOTIFYPARENT` 0x20 @0xA8).
- Bool-returning inline used as a condition: `return !(x & 1)` -> `not al; test al,1` (SwampSceneNode::IsValid on `_RenderingOptions` @0x88, NodeOptions::Invalid; SoundNode::Update).
- `feof(fp)` (VC7.1 macro) -> `test byte [eax+0Ch],10h` (`_iobuf::_flag & _IOEOF`) (SwampInput::GetRecord).
- False positives: `test ah,41h/5/1` after `fnstsw` = x87 compare status, not a flag field (AACylinder::Overlaps); the detector tags them bittest.
- Source rule: look up the member in TPI first; LF_BITFIELD would mean a C bitfield (expect `bt`/shift+and), a plain integer means `x & ENUM` (D78: mask from the enum, never a bare literal when an enum exists).
- opt: /O2 control fails on MoviePlayer, SoundNode::Update, SwampGeometry; /Oy- fails on SwampGeometry only; a lone `test` fn passes /O2 (DoNotifyParent), so pick a discriminating row.
- Unit without /EH including VC7.1 `<string>`: C4530 kills the build; `#pragma warning(disable: 4530)` marked `// !FAKE:` (SoundNode.cpp).
