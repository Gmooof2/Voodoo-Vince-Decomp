# Fold-VA relink after the 3.4 T6.1 lift: one template arg per fold VA; decl-only specialization (3.4 T10.c2)
- The T6.1 lift (C0136) slots our folded-member COMDAT copy at its pin VA, but only one copy per fold VA, or several with the same name. VinceSwing.obj and Hannah.obj both emit `~ResourceHandle<AnimationResource>` at 0x292A6: pass. Two different template args at one fold VA are refused: `our ranges overlap at 0x<va>` (HealthUI's `??_I` names `~ResourceHandle<TextureResource>` at 0x292A6; SwampRenderer emitting TextureResource and VertexShaderResource `operator=` at 0x1819A). Lifting it needs relink, which is verifier-locked: a review task.
- Fix in source when the extra copy is not needed: a declaration-only explicit specialization, `template <> ResourceHandle<VertexShaderResource>& ResourceHandle<VertexShaderResource>::operator=(const ResourceHandle<VertexShaderResource>&);`. It stops the visible generic body from emitting that instantiation, and the caller keeps an external call. A defined `template<>` is not selectany, so it fails the gate's `*objects* extra`.
- The gate prints only `FAIL globals: relink`. Get the reason by calling `relink.gate_check` from a scratch script.
- Retail disasm of a delinked unit: `llvm-objdump -d -r --no-show-raw-insn derived/delink/retail-2953b782/<unit>.obj`.
- objdiff pairs functions by symbol name, and the retail cut gives each fold VA one alias name: any other alias gives bytes equal with `objdiff: None`. A fold-alias row finishes only when its owning unit instantiates the cut's name (`llvm-nm derived/delink/retail-2953b782/<unit>.obj`). Otherwise it gets a verdict, or a cut-name decision (pin/relink, a review task) (3.4 T10.c3: Lantern FrameTick, Blender vector<MapStop>, Onion operator=).
- `#pragma intrinsic(memcpy)` when retail inlines `rep movs` for a memcpy (CreateVertexBuffer 58.08 without it).
